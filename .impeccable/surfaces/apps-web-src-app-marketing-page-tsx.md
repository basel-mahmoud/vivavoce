# Surface brief: marketing home (/)

Scope: the marketing home page, Persuade mode. Inner marketing pages inherit
the same world through shared components. Round 2 (redesign of the v5 room):
the owner pinned the 3D panel of judges and rejected its execution ("the 3d
objects representing judges look crappy and super basic"); they asked for
something exceptional with standout components and animation.

Audience: university students facing oral exams and vivas (medicine, law,
engineering), plus job seekers and presenters. Job: believe this is a real
sparring partner that marks spoken answers, try it, then join early access or
install the Android beta. Proof: the live marking engine (mic + Gemini) and the
five-axis rubric. Constraints: scores are guidance, not grades, stated wherever
scores appear; example rounds are labelled as examples; no em-dashes in copy.

Memorable moment: the panel listens to you. While you answer, all five glass
faces show your voice; when you stop, it collapses into five marks and the
weakest examiner leans in with the follow-up.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The viva room seen from the hot seat, and the panel visibly listens.
Five AI examiners, one per axis, show on their glass faces what they are doing
with your answer: listening, conferring, marking, asking. Refuses the voice-orb
dark landing, the centred headline over a feature grid, and pawn mascots.

OWN-WORLD: The exam script. Blue ink (cobalt) is the candidate and every
candidate action; red pen (vermilion) is the examiner and only the examiner's
marks; butter is the highlighter; coal is print; a cool exam-paper ground with
faint blue rules and a red margin rule as motif. Examiners are five studio
instruments (bench instrument in half-moon specs, butter bulb with a loupe,
tiered stack with a binder clip, stone metronome, cobalt pebble in a headset)
in ceramic, powder coat and lacquer, black-glass visors with champagne lips,
paper mittens on short conduit arms, bevelled-coin paddles. A real set: a
cyclorama wall that catches soft shadows, a satin cobalt bench, a lamp pool.
Archivo expanded for display, JetBrains Mono only for marks. Night: blue-black
paper, the lamp on, the faces the only glow.

STORY: An example round plays in the room; scrolling cuts wide, medium, close
and over-the-shoulder while each axis explains itself in red-pen margin notes;
then the visitor answers for real and the same panel marks them; modes, loop,
subjects, use cases and privacy each take their own form; close on an
admission slip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ~42%: "Say it out loud before it counts." in two lines,
"before" circled in red pen, one support line, "Answer a question" (cobalt
ink, primary) and "Get early access". Right ~58%, bleeding off the edge: the
panel behind the bench under a lamp pool, Structure speaking (vermilion
waveform face, 48 raised), the other four turned toward it, every mark
legible, an "Example round" label.

FORM: The viva room v2, owner-pinned; the examiner form was chosen by a
four-way rendered prototype panel (visors won; toys, busts and scholars
declined). Seed key e2124715. Raises: from toys, the lamp pool, paddles laid
face-down on the bench at rest, tactile material detail and exam props; from
busts, the shadow-catching wall, a staggered turn toward the speaker and
readable inlaid axis names; from scholars, per-scheme colour calibration,
gestures and depth of field on close-ups.

SIGNATURE: "The panel marks your answer." Hold to answer; the visors show your
live waveform; it collapses into the mark disc; paddles rise and roll to their
scores; the red pen marks your transcript; the verdict board flips to Fix first.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
