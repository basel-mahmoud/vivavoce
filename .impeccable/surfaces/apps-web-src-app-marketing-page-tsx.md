# Surface brief: marketing home (/)

Scope: the marketing home page, Persuade mode. Inner marketing pages inherit
the same world through shared components.

Audience: university students facing oral exams and vivas (medicine, law,
engineering), plus job seekers and presenters. Job: believe this is a real
sparring partner that marks spoken answers, try it, then join early access or
install the Android beta. Proof: the live marking engine (mic + Gemini) and the
five-axis rubric. Constraints: scores are guidance, not grades, stated wherever
scores appear; example rounds are labelled as examples; no em-dashes in copy.

Memorable moment: a panel of five examiners across a cobalt table flips up
score paddles, and the weakest one leans in to ask the follow-up.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the viva room seen from the hot seat. A panel of five
examiners, one per scoring axis, sits across a long table; the visitor is the
candidate. Refuses the category default of a dark AI landing with a glowing
voice orb, a centered headline and a feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Porcelain floor, a saturated cobalt table, five clay-matte examiner
figures in ink, cobalt, butter, paper and vermilion, round score paddles set in
JetBrains Mono, a desk microphone facing the visitor. Archivo Black expanded
for every headline. Flat colour fields, soft contact shadows, no glow, no glass,
no gradient text. State is carried by pose and height as well as colour. Lights
off (dark scheme) is the same room with the lamp on.

STORY: First viewport: an example round plays (question, answer, paddles).
Scrolling walks the camera down the panel, one examiner per axis. Then the
visitor answers for real in the live engine, sees the six modes, the loop, the
use cases, and closes on early access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third: the headline "Say it out loud before it counts."
at display scale, one sentence of support, primary action "Answer a question"
(jumps to the live engine) and "Get early access". Right two thirds, bleeding
to the edge: the 3D room, camera at the candidate's eye line, five figures
behind name placards, paddles raised with example marks, a question card and
an "Example round" label.

FORM: The viva room (assigned candidate 3 of 7). Seed key e2124715. Raises:
from the console-atmosphere challenger, the focused examiner rises and swells
while the rest recede; from the paper automata, one scroll timeline drives the
whole panel and only the figure in motion wears vermilion; from the VU bridge,
the live mic meter uses real AnalyserNode levels with needle ballistics; from
the emission rail, axis state reads in shape and height, never colour alone.
